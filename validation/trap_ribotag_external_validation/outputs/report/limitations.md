# Limitations

1. **GSE210071 is a transcription-inhibited system.** Every compartment sample received triptolide for 3 h before fractionation, in embryonic mixed cortical cultures. Nascent nuclear RNA is depleted, so nuclear-vs-cytosol ratios of short-lived nuclear transcripts are distorted. Concordance here validates the direction of stable-transcript compartmentalization, not the steady-state nuclear transcriptome. Malat1 and Neat1 are absent from the processed table.
2. **Unit mismatch.** Model deltas are differences of mean log1p counts and are positive for ~97% of genes; external log ratios are normalized and centered. Only rank statistics and centered sign agreement are interpretable; slopes are not calibration.
3. **Global rank concordance is not method-discriminative.** On GSE210071 all seven methods, including the scVI family, give rho 0.37 to 0.41; the global cell-nucleus axis is captured by any method that shifts nuclei toward cells. Method differences appear in AUROC and in the cell-type-resolved H2/H3 tests, where ImputeVAE reaches the real-cell ceiling.
4. **GSE121199 has no matched cell type** (striatal MSNs vs cortical model), the D1 nuclear libraries are shallow (four of six under 5 M reads) and the D1 contrast fails for the ground truth as well as the model. Female RiboTag samples were excluded. RiboTag-vs-whole-cell is positive here, unlike IP/input in bulk cortex, likely because FACS-sorted whole neurons lose neuropil that RiboTag captures.
5. **RiboTag/TRAP is ribosome-associated RNA, not cytoplasm.** H2 and H3 are supporting evidence. Enrichment-type contrasts (IP/input, IP/UB) are weakly or negatively related to the delta for the ground truth itself.
6. **GSE263082 pools cortex and hippocampus**, batch2 is pre-filtered to 11,808 genes, and batch outlier flags (one sample each in batches 2 to 4) were reported, not excluded. GSE13379 has n = 1 unbound per population and microarray dynamic-range compression.
7. **Sub-cytoplasmic localization is not validated**: the ML-minus-CB TRAP enrichment axis in CA1 is uncorrelated with the delta.
8. **Mouse only.** No human generalization is claimed. MERFISH/FISH and paired human data remain separate next steps.
9. **Cell-type mapping choices** for GSE13379 populations (e.g. Cck/Pnoc to the inhibitory pool, Olig2 to Oligo) are approximations and documented in `scripts/09_secondary_datasets.py`.
