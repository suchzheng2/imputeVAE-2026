# Executive summary: external nucleus-to-whole-cell validation with compartment and TRAP/RiboTag data

**Date:** 2026-09-18. **Model:** ImputeVAE full-data cVAE (`run/run_2026-07-18-19-40-51_570984`), frozen. **Datasets:** GSE210071, GSE121199, GSE263082 (+ GSE77243 rerun on the same pipeline), GSE174303, GSE13379. All mouse. Every number below has the MOp ground-truth delta (real cell minus nucleus) computed alongside it.

**1. Does ImputeVAE recover the direction of nuclear-to-whole-cell shifts in independent compartment data?** Yes, at the level the biology supports. In biochemically fractionated cortical cultures (GSE210071) the predicted delta ranks genes by cytosol-minus-nucleus log ratio with Spearman rho 0.38 (95% CI 0.37 to 0.40) and by whole-cell-minus-nucleus with rho 0.41 (0.39 to 0.43); AUROC for separating cytosol-enriched from nucleus-enriched genes is 0.80 and 0.82. The abundance-matched null is centered at zero (permutation p = 0.001). The ground-truth MOp delta gives rho 0.37 and 0.40 on the same genes, so the model reaches the ceiling set by the cross-system biology. Every sensitivity filter (low expression, mitochondrial, ribosomal, immediate-early, nuclear ncRNA class, coding only, all combined) leaves rho between 0.33 and 0.41. In FACS-sorted striatal neurons (GSE121199, no matched cell type) the D2 whole-cell-vs-nuclear contrast gives rho 0.50 and AUROC 0.81; the D1 contrast fails (rho -0.10) because four of six D1 nuclear libraries have under 5 million reads, and the ground-truth delta fails identically, so this is an external data-quality limit rather than a model failure.

**2. Does imputation move Pvalb and Sst profiles closer to external RiboTag profiles?** Yes, and almost to the ceiling. Against pooled baseline Pvalb RiboTag (GSE263082, three batches, 8 replicates) the Spearman correlation rises from 0.43 (raw nucleus) to 0.73 (imputed), with real MOp Pvalb cells at 0.74; for Sst it rises from 0.45 to 0.73 with real cells at 0.73. Improvement CIs exclude zero in every batch (gene bootstrap and replicate bootstrap). The same pipeline on GSE77243 (different lab) gives 0.50 to 0.78 (Pvalb) and 0.50 to 0.78 (Sst). Imputed Pvalb matches Pvalb RiboTag better than Sst RiboTag and vice versa, mirroring real cells.

**3. Which conclusions replicate across datasets?** Rank concordance of the predicted delta with direct compartment ratios (two fractionation designs, two tissues, cultured and adult). The near-ceiling profile improvement for Pvalb and Sst in two independent RiboTag studies. The strong RiboTag-abundance-versus-delta relation (rho 0.74 to 0.80 with sequencing, 0.24 to 0.40 with microarray bacTRAP across nine cortical populations; partial correlation given nucleus expression 0.69 to 0.74). The property mismatch for enrichment-type contrasts: IP-over-input and IP-over-unbound ratios correlate weakly or negatively with the delta (-0.27 to +0.24), for the ground truth as much as for the model.

**4. Which genes or contexts fail?** Camk2n1 is flat in cultured-cell fractionation (log2 ratio -0.05) despite a large predicted delta (+3.8), while it is strongly whole-cell-enriched in adult striatal neurons (+1.3) and RiboTag (+4.1); the culture context, not the model, disagrees. Meg3 and Snhg11 are nuclear in every external dataset but the model predicts Meg3 positive globally (it is nuclear only in glia in MOp ground truth), so Meg3 is a genuine model-versus-external disagreement in mixed cultures. Sst is nuclear-depleted in whole-cell FACS MSNs (-4.5) but RiboTag-enriched (+3.2); the model's small positive delta for Sst is concordant with fractionation and RiboTag but not with sorted whole cells. Malat1 and Neat1 could not be tested in GSE210071 (absent from the processed table); Neat1 and Pvalb are absent from the model gene set. Global rank concordance (H1) does not separate ImputeVAE from CT-mean, linear regression, or the scVI family (all rho 0.37 to 0.41 on GSE210071); only the AUROC and the cell-type-resolved H2/H3 tests distinguish methods.

**5. What can be claimed now, and what still needs MERFISH/FISH or paired human data?** Claim now: the learned transformation is concordant with independent biochemical compartment measurements and improves similarity of nuclear profiles to ribosome-associated profiles of matched interneuron types, in mouse, non-circularly. Not claimable: single-cell-level localization, sub-cytoplasmic (dendritic) localization (GSE174303 ML-minus-CB enrichment is uncorrelated with the delta, rho -0.04), or any human generalization. Those require spatial/FISH validation and paired human data, which remain separate next steps.
