# Methods

## Model inputs
Per-cell-type pseudobulks (mean log1p counts) of 40,166 v3 MOp nuclei, their imputed whole-cell counterparts (`run/cvae_full_eval/imputed_cell_from_nucleus.npy`, label alignment 100%), and 71,117 real v3 cells, over the 10,134 model genes (`scripts/01_build_model_pseudobulk.py`). predicted_delta = imputed - nucleus; ground-truth delta = real cell - nucleus; identical to `compartment_classification/gt_delta_per_ct.csv` (r = 1.000). Pooled columns: ALL (all nuclei), POOL_neuronal (12 neuronal CTs), POOL_inhibitory (5), POOL_excitatory (7), POOL_nonneuronal (5). A validation-split version (8,033 nuclei) reproduced all H2 results to the third decimal. Deltas are differences of mean log1p counts (not library-size normalized), so they are positive for ~97% of genes; external contrasts are log2 ratios of normalized values. Rank statistics are therefore primary; slopes are reported but not interpreted; sign agreement is reported both raw and after median-centering both vectors.

## External data
Downloaded 2026-09-17 from GEO (`outputs/data_manifest.tsv`, SHA-256 recorded). Metadata parsed from series-matrix sample records and reconciled with matrix columns (`outputs/metadata/`). Gene symbols harmonized to the model set (`outputs/gene_mapping/mapping_summary.tsv`): Ensembl IDs via the project BioMart export; GSE263082 batch3-5 embedded symbols with 110 renamed genes rescued through BioMart; GSE13379 probes via GPL1261 (median log2 GCRMA across probes); ambiguous multi-symbol rows dropped; duplicate symbols summed (counts) or median-collapsed (FPKM).

- **GSE210071**: log2(FPKM + 0.1); expressed = mean FPKM >= 1 in any compartment (11,553 genes); paired differences across the three cultures (S1, S2, S4), paired t (df = 2), BH FDR. All compartment samples were triptolide-treated for 3 h; unstimulated = primary, bicuculline = sensitivity.
- **GSE121199**: edgeR filterByExpr + TMM, limma-voom, design ~0 + assay_subtype, eBayes robust. Male only; `wc_D2_M_3_L8` summed into `wc_D2_M_3` (technical re-sequencing, rho 0.995); `wc_D1_M_1_2` excluded (sensitivity run includes it). Contrasts within D1, within D2, subtype-averaged; RiboTag vs nuclear and RiboTag vs whole cell.
- **GSE263082**: per batch filterByExpr + TMM + logCPM (prior 2) on wild-type samples; baseline (saline/homecage) means per batch; META = equal-weight mean over batches on genes present in all batches; combined limma fit ~0 + celltype + batch for Pvalb-vs-Sst. Nr4a1 cKO, PTZ, CFC excluded from primary. Outliers flagged within cell type (informational).
- **GSE77243**: Greenberg supplementary table, `Pv_Std_B*`/`Sst_Std_B*` replicates, log2(x + 1).
- **GSE174303**: WT only, limma-voom ~0 + group + replicate; TRAP-input within CB and ML; ML-minus-CB enrichment difference; input ML-vs-CB.
- **GSE13379**: series-matrix GCRMA (linear, log2-transformed), cortex populations, TRAP mean of 3 replicates; IP/UB with n = 1 (or 2) unbound, descriptive.

## Statistics
Per contrast: n overlapping genes; Spearman and Pearson with 1,000-iteration gene bootstrap CIs; Theil-Sen slope on a 3,000-gene subsample; sign agreement on externally reliable genes (|log2FC| > 0.5, FDR < 0.1) raw and median-centered; AUROC of the predicted delta for externally cytosol-enriched vs nucleus-enriched genes; Fisher enrichment of the top/bottom predicted decile; abundance-matched permutation null (predicted delta permuted within deciles of nucleus expression, 1,000 iterations, seed 42). H2: delta_similarity with paired gene bootstrap and external replicate bootstrap (1,000 each). H3: Spearman of RiboTag abundance vs predicted delta, partial Spearman given nucleus expression, enrichment of top 5/10/20% predicted-positive genes among the top 20% RiboTag-expressed genes vs the matched null. BH within families; primary vs sensitivity labeled in every table. Other methods (CT-mean, linear regression, scVI, scANVI, scGen, scButterfly) taken from `compartment_classification/predicted_deltas_global.csv` for global contrasts. R 4.3.2 (edgeR 4.0, limma 3.58; `outputs/logs/*sessionInfo.txt`), Python 3.12 (`outputs/logs/run_log.md`).
