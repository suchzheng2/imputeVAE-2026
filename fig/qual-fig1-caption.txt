Figure 1. The empirical nucleus-cell relationship varies across cell types. (A) Pearson correlation across 10,134 genes between mean whole-cell and nuclear expression profiles for each of the 17 cell types represented in both modalities. (B) Mean absolute empirical cell-minus-nucleus expression shift across genes for each cell type. Correlation measures preservation of the relative expression pattern, whereas mean absolute shift measures the magnitude of the nucleus-cell difference. Points show cell-type estimates; intervals show 95% bootstrap confidence intervals obtained by independently resampling cells and nuclei within each cell type. Neuronal and non-neuronal populations are shown in blue and green, respectively.
