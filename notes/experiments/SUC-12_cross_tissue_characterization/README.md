# SUC-12: Prerequisite Study — Characterize Nucleus-to-Cell Difference Across Tissues and Species

**Linear issue:** [SUC-12](https://linear.app/suchen/issue/SUC-12/prerequisite-study-characterize-nucleus-to-cell-difference-across)
**Status:** Backlog
**Priority:** High — prerequisite for SUC-11

## Motivation

Before implementing cross-species or cross-tissue transfer (SUC-11), it is necessary to empirically characterize the nucleus-to-cell expression difference across tissues and species. The entire generalization story depends on how conserved this difference is.

## Approach

Analysis study using public data — no new model training. Compare nucleus-to-cell differences across:
1. Multiple mouse brain regions
2. Mouse vs human brain
3. Brain vs other tissues (e.g., heart, liver)
