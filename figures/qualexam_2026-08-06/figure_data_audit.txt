4. VALUE SCALE
===============
All evaluation data is in log1p(raw counts) scale.
load_raw_data() default: normalization="log1p" -> np.log1p(X)
The imputed prediction arrays are also in log1p scale (ImputeVAE outputs log1p).
Delta = mean(imputed or cell) - mean(nucleus) in log1p space.

5. AVAILABLE METRICS BY SOURCE
================================

A. Eval JSONs (per method):
   - gene_corr: median, mean, std, pct thresholds
   - r_squared: median, mean (CT-mean level R²)
   - shift: mean_shift, median_shift
   - gene_mse: median, mean
   - variance_ratio: median_ratio, mean_ratio (SUMMARY ONLY, no per-CT)
   - delta_gt: overall_corr, overall_r2, per_ct_corr (17 CTs), per_ct_r2
   - wasserstein: median, mean, n_genes=200
   - compartment: ribosomal/cytoplasmic/nuclear_lncrna sign fractions
   Available for: cvae_full_v2, ctmean_v2, scvi_v2, scanvi, scgen,
                  scbutterfly, linreg

B. compartment_classification/ CSVs:
   - gt_delta_global.csv: gene, cell_mean, nuc_mean, gt_delta (10134 genes)
   - gt_delta_per_ct.csv: gene x 17 CTs, ground truth delta (10134 genes)
   - predicted_deltas_global.csv: gene x 8 methods, predicted delta (10134 genes)
   - method_comparison.csv: AUROC/AUPRC/accuracy etc at tau=0,0.1,0.2
   - per_ct_method_auroc.csv: per-CT AUROC for each method (17 CTs x 8 methods)
   - ct_counts.json: cell/nuclei counts per CT

C. Per-CT predicted deltas for COMPARISON METHODS: NOT AVAILABLE
   Only gt_delta_per_ct.csv exists. There is no per-CT predicted delta for
   scGen, scButterfly, scVI, etc. Only the global (averaged across all CTs)
   predicted delta is available.
